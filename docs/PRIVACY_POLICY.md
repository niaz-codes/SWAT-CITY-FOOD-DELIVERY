# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 21, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 21, 2026

## Introduction
Swat City Food Delivery ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Swat City Food Delivery mobile application and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
We collect information that you provide directly to us:
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, phone number, password
- **Profile Information**: Profile picture (for riders)
- **Delivery Information**: Delivery addresses, location data
- **Payment Information**: Payment method details (processed securely through payment gateways)
- **Identity Information**: CNIC details (for rider verification only)

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Location Data**: We collect your precise or approximate location to facilitate deliveries and rider tracking
- **Device Information**: Device tokens for push notifications
- **Usage Information**: Order history, app interactions
- **Log Information**: Access logs, error logs

## How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your account
- Process and fulfill food orders
- Facilitate payments
- Connect customers with restaurants and riders
- Provide real-time order tracking
- Send notifications about orders and account updates
- Improve our services and user experience
- Ensure platform safety and prevent fraud
- Comply with legal obligations

## Third-Party Services
We may share your information with:
- **Supabase**: Database, authentication, and storage services
- **Payment Gateways**: Easypaisa, JazzCash for payment processing
- **Map Services**: For location and routing (as used in the app)
- **Push Notification Services**: Expo Push Notifications

## Data Security
We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures including:
- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Secure token storage on mobile devices
- Row Level Security (RLS) on database
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and authentication

## Data Retention
We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account at any time.

## Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to processing of your data
- Data portability
- Withdraw consent

## Account Deletion
You can delete your account by:
1. Using the "Delete my account" button in the app (Profile > Settings)
2. Submitting a request through our web deletion page

Upon deletion, we will remove or anonymize your personal data in accordance with our data retention policy, except where we are required to retain it by law.

## Children's Privacy
Our service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
