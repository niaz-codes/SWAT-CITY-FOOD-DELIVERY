# Glossary

- **COD** — Cash on Delivery
- **PKR / Rs.** — Pakistani Rupee
- **Rider** — delivery person
- **Zone** — serviceable delivery area
- **Order status** — stage in `ORDER_FLOW.md`
- **Payout** — money paid to restaurant/rider
- **Settlement** — reconciliation of COD cash
- **OTP** — one-time password
- **Idempotency** — repeating a request has no extra effect
