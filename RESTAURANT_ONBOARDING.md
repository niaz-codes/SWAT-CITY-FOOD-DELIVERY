# Restaurant Onboarding

1. Application: name, owner, phone, address, area, opening hours
2. Documents: CNIC, optional NTN/licence
3. Menu upload with photos, prices, categories
4. Agreement: commission %, payout schedule
5. Admin approval -> account activated
6. Test order before going live

## Ongoing

- Keep menu and availability current
- Prep-time accuracy tracked
- Weekly/bi-weekly payout statements
